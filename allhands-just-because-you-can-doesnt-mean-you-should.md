
# Request for comments: All-Hands change 

TL;DR: Push our all-hands demos to be impact based, so demos are "Look at the impact I've made with doing X", not just "Behold, X".

## Problem statement

The bar for software has moved; PostHog's superpower was always "shipping & shipping fast [for the users]" but since AI has made the shipping & shipping fast and far too easy, and I feel we're slipping and losing the emphasis on "[for the users]".
#### "Just because you can, doesn’t mean you should"

## Success criteria

_How do we know if this is successful (i.e. metrics, customer feedback), what's out of scope, what makes this ambitious?_
We know this is successful when; 
* Our people see results in a demo, either positive or negative. 
  * Positive: a happy users tweet, metrics for that feature, and it's use, which all backup their reasoning for building said feature/thing.
  * Negative: conversion dropped, people hated it, tons of support tickets. Learning from mistakes is just as important as learning from successes.
* Our people feel like our all-hands demos are impact & outcome based, not just "show and tell". 
* Our people feel our bar for what we do is higher
* Our presenter feels accoplished in what they've done for PostHog and our users, or that they've shared their learnings with others.
* Our people watching the demo feels empowered/inspired to strive for better outcomes themselves.

## Context

* **What are our competitors doing**: Most of our competitors have a higher bar in quality, and we need to move toward quality > quantity.
* **External motiviations**: With the ease of creating new features, it's becoming too easy for our teams to push features that are; a) not neeeded b) not measured &, c) not maintained
